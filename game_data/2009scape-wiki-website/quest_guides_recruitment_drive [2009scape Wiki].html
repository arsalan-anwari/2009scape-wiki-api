  • 1 quest point
  • 1,000 Prayer XP
  • 1,000 Herblore XP
  • 1,000 Agility XP

Start

1. Start by talk-to Sir Amik Varze.

Before the next step, approach the bank and leave all your belongings.

2. Now run to Falador Park, and talk-to grandpa who sits on the bench and drink tea, he's name is Sir Tiffany Cashien.

3. Choose that you are ready for the test, even if you are not, it will be fun.

*Note for puzzles below, they are in random order so you will not get exactly the order of this guide unless you're lucky.*
