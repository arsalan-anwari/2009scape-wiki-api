Start Point

Lumbridge Castle - Kitchen.

Requirements

  • - Bucket of milk or Empty Bucket
  • - Pot of flour or Empty pot
  • - Egg

Ratings

  • Difficulty: Beginner.
  • Length: Short.

Reward

  • 1 Quest Point,
  • 300 Cooking XP,
  • Ability to use the Lumbridge Kitchen range.

Experience received from a quest as a reward is multiplied by your xp rate

Getting Started

After speaking to the chef, you will need to collect three ingredients for his cake: a bucket of milk, an egg and a pot of flour. By the way, you can take an empty pot from the table next to him

Getting the Milk

1. Go to the General Store - north of castle, and buy an empty bucket.

2. From the General Store run east to a cow farm and use the bucket on the dairy cow to get milk:

Getting the Egg

3. Next to the cow farm is a chicken farm; grab an egg from there:

Getting the Flour

4. Go back and cross the bridge to get you next to the furnace, then run north to the mill.

It is marked on the map with this sign.

however, before entering the mill, collect the wheat that is on the field on the left side. Then go inside the mill and take the empty pot from the table:

5. Go to the very top of the mill and use wheat on the hopper and next to it pull the lever and go back downstairs, then collect the flour using empty pot on flour.

Back to the Chef

6. Bring all three items back to the chef in the Lumbridge Castle Kitchen to complete the quest.
