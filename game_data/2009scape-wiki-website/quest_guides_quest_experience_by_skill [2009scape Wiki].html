Quest Experience by Skill

This guide provides detailed tables outlining the quests in 2009Scape that reward experience in each of the skills.

Each skill is listed alphabetically, and the quests within each table are sorted by the amount of experience awarded, from highest to lowest, as well as the number of quest points.

Whether you’re a veteran player looking to optimize your experience gains or a new adventurer trying to plan your path, I hope these tables are a helpful reference for you!

- Rustfox
