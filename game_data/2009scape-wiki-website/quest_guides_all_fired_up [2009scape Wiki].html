Start Point

  • Varrock Palace.

Requirements

  • 43 Firemaking
  • Completion of the following quests:
    • Priest in Perilplugin-autotooltip__default plugin-autotooltip_bigquest_guides:priest_in_peril

      There's something going on on the eastern border of Varrock and King Roald needs someone to look into it. The way to Varrock is protected from the dangers of the eastern reaches by the hard work of the monks on the shores of the River Salve. Will you take up the responsibility to make sure their critical work has not been interrupted?

Items Required

  • - Tinderbox
  • - 45x Logs of any type (2 sets of 20 and 1 set of 5)

Ratings

  • Difficulty: Intermediate
  • Length: Short to Medium

Reward

  • 1 Quest Point
  • 20,000 coins
  • 5,500 Firemaking XP
  • Access to the All Fired Up minigame

Experience received from a quest as a reward is multiplied by your xp rate

Getting Started

  • Items required: None.

Talk to King Roald at Varrock Palace to begin the quest.

Tending the First Beacon

  • Items required: Tinderbox, 20 Logs (must all be of same type).

Go east from Varrock and speak with Blaze Sharpeye in Silvarea near the beacon just south of the Paterdomus Temple. It is recommended to collect the 20 Logs (must all be of same type) on the way.

Place 20 Logs in the beacon next to Blaze Sharpeye and light it with a Tinderbox. Then talk to Blaze Sharpeye again.

Tending the Second Beacon

  • Items required: Tinderbox, 20 Logs (must all be of same type).

Go west from Blaze Sharpeye to the second beacon near the Odd Old Man and speak with Squire Fyre. It is recommended to collect the 20 Logs (must all be of same type) on the way from the trees southwest of Squire Fyre. Place 20 Logs in the beacon next to Squire Fyre and light it with a Tinderbox.

Tending the First Beacon (Again)

  • Items required: 5 Logs (must all be of same type).

Head back to Blaze Sharpeye and speak with him. It is recommended to collect the 5 Logs (must all be of same type) from the trees just east of Blaze Sharpeye. Add 5 Logs (must all be the same type) to the beacon next to Blaze Sharpeye. Then talk to Blaze Sharpeye again.

Finishing Up

  • Items required: None.

Head back to Varrock Palace and speak with King Roald to complete the quest.

Rewards


This Quest also gives access to the All Fired Up mini game wherein you get the oportuniaty to try for getting some nice rewards. You can find the guide to this under our Activity Guides or you can click HEREplugin-autotooltip__default plugin-autotooltip_bigminigames:all_fired_up

Intro:

King Roald of Varrock has built a beacon fire warning system. Help him test it out and gain rewards to aid you in your Firemaking skills. This mini-game is dangerous because it requires traveling through high level of the wilderness. Additional requirements are needed to light all 14 beacons.
