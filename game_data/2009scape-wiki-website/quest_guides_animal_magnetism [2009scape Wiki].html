Start Point

Draynor Manor, north of Draynor Village.

Quest Required

  • The Restless Ghostplugin-autotooltip__default plugin-autotooltip_bigStart Point

    Church south-east of Lumbridge Castle.

    Required

    * The ability to defeat a level 13 skeleton.

    Ratings

    * Difficulty: Beginner. * Length: Short.

    Reward

    * 1 Quest Point * 1,125 Prayer XP * Ghostspeak amulet Experience received from a quest as a reward is multiplied by your xp rate
  • Ernest the Chickenplugin-autotooltip__default plugin-autotooltip_bigStart Point

    South of Draynor Manor.

    Requirements

    * None

    Ratings

    * Difficulty: Beginner. * Length: Short.

    Reward

    * 4 Quest Points * 300 Coins

    Start

    1. Talk to Veronica standing just outside the courtyard of the Draynor Manor.
  • Priest in Perilplugin-autotooltip__default plugin-autotooltip_bigquest_guides:priest_in_peril

    There's something going on on the eastern border of Varrock and King Roald needs someone to look into it. The way to Varrock is protected from the dangers of the eastern reaches by the hard work of the monks on the shores of the River Salve. Will you take up the responsibility to make sure their critical work has not been interrupted?

Skill Requirements

  • 18 Slayer level
  • 19 Crafting level
  • 30 Ranged level
  • 35 Woodcutting level

Items Required

  • - Mithril axe
  • - 5 x Iron bar
  • - Hammer
  • - Holy symbol
  • - Hard leather
  • - Polished buttons (You will get them by killing H.A.M Guards or pickpocketing them.)
  • - Ghostspeak amulet (from The Restless Ghost quest)
  • - 20 Ecto-tokens (or 4 bones, 4 buckets and 4 pots to gain the tokens)

plugin-autotooltip__default plugin-autotooltip_bigBasic Information

* Ecto-Tokens can be earned by Worshipping the Ectofuntus. * The Ectofuntus is in a temple located to the north of Port Phasmatys.

The Ectophial flask allows teleport to Ectofuntus.

You can get it from Velorina - is a female ghost in the north-east part of Port Phasmatys. Once the player teleports, they will automatically refill it.

Optional Items

  • - Amulet of Glory
  • - Games Necklace
  • - Teleport to your empty grass field

Ratings

  • Difficulty: hardmode.
  • Length: a little longer than shorter.

Reward

  • 1 Quest point
  • 1000 Crafting XP
  • 1000 Fletching XP
  • 1000 Slayer XP
  • 2500 Woodcutting XP
  • Ava's attractor if your Ranged level is less than 50, or Ava's accumulator if your Ranged level is 50 or higher
  • The ability to trade with Ava and purchase her devices

Experience received from a quest as a reward is multiplied by your xp rate

Getting Started

1. Talk to Ava:

2. Travel to the Undead Chicken Farm west of the Ectofuntus:

(temple is located to the north of Port Phasmatys)

3. talk with Alice:

4. Wield Ghostspeak amulet and talk with Alice Husband, then return to alice and again to alice husband, so do it three times.

5. Now we go meet with The Old Crone to get amulet. Old Crone is located east of the Slayer Tower.

6. Talk to The Old crone:

7. After talk you will get the amulet.

8. Deliver the amulet to the Alice husband at the farm, and talk to him again.

9. Hold on. Cut-scene starts:

10. Talk with Alice Husband to get 2 Indead chickens for 20 ecto-tokens:

11. Back to Ava located in Draynor Manor, will tell you to talk to the witch:

12. Talk with Witch, twice to get the selected iron (for your 5 iron bars),

(she is located at the room next to Ava):

13. Run to Rimmington mining site:

(you can use the teleport to your cottage)

14. Stand to the north as in the image below and use Hammer on Selected iron to get Bar magnet:

15. Back to Ava,

(She will tell you to cut down a tree, go outside the manor and then go in front of the front door to try to cut down the tree she told you to cut down):

16. Get back to her and talk again, she will tell you to go to Turael in Burthope:

(use games necklace and choose first option → burthope)

17. Talk to Turael, he will grant you a blessed axe in exchange for a mithril axe and a holy symbol.

18. Go back to Draynor Manor to talk with Ava, but before enter the mansion; cut down the tree you couldn't cut down before.

19. Ok. Now enter the mansion and talk to Ava. She give you Research notes:

20. You can translate these notes by selecting the correct buttons that appear on screen.

(Click the first, third, fourth, sixth, seventh, and eight circles.)

21. If you managed to do it, talk to Ava again, after talk use the received a pattern on Hard leather to get a Container:

22. Hand the container over to Ava to complete the quest.
