Requirements

* None

* Have a couple slots of inventory for items.

Steps

Start

Go to the house west of the Ardougne zoo. Talk to Ceril Carnillean.

Ask “What's Wrong” and hear him complain about black clothed cultists from the forest to the south, by a cave entrance, and how they stole a suit of armour. Also pay attention when he says that the new butler “makes a nice broth.”

The cave entrance is just east of the clocktower.

Right click and speak to Clivet. Do not attack him accidentally.

Clivet will claim that the Carnilleans are traitors to Zamorak and earned their home and position by betraying Lord Hazeel. You will be given an opportunity to join the cult at this point.

This is a branching path. You must pick to help the cultists or help the Carnilleans.

Path: Helping the cultists

Tell Clivet “Count me in”. He will give you poison and order you to poison their food

Go back to the manor, go into the basement, and use the poison on the cooking range.

Your quest log will update to say that you only managed to kill their dog. Congrats on that, hero!

Note: if you want to take the opportunity to speak to the family members and hear how much their dog meant to them, now's your chance hero!

Talk to Clivet and get the The Sign of Hazeel amulet. You now need to do a valve puzzle.

Take a note of how Hazeel's Mark is a Z, but that the middle dot on the bottom of the Z is much flatter than the other dots. Almost like trying to make a Z with five dots instead of six.

Exit the cave. You'll have to find five sewer valves around and turn them the correct way. The dialogue is the same no matter which way you turn them, so there is no easy way to know if you are doing this right.

The one west of the clock tower turns to the right.

The one north of the clock tower goes right.

The one north of the Hazeel cult cave turns left.

The one south of penguins turns right.

The one at the fork in the road turns right.

Go back down the cave and notice the new raft you can board. Make sure you're wearing Hazeel's mark.

At the destination, find Alomone at the end and talk to him.

Alomone will say that a scroll is needed to resurrect Hazeel, and that the butler is a spy that has not succeeded in finding the scroll that's hidden in the house.

Take the raft back, go to the house. If you talk to the butler jones with the amulet in your inventory or equiped, he will confirm himself to be a spy and also that the scroll is for sure in the house.

Go to the basement and search the crates until you find a rusty key.

Go upstairs and knock-at the wall to the ladder. Keep doing this until you get through.

Search the chest upstairs for the scroll of hazeel.

Go back to Alomone with the scroll in hand. If he's not here, wait for him to respawn as the Carnellian side requires a different adventurer to kill him. Click through all the dialogue.

Quest complete!

You can drop the chest key for inventory/bank space.
